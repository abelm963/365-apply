How it works

  1. Give your name, cellphone and the amount, and upload your ID, latest payslip and 3 months' bank statements.
  2. Send us the WhatsApp message the page gives you, so we know the number is yours.
  3. We check your documents and confirm the amount and repayment date with you on WhatsApp.
  4. The money is paid into your bank account.
  5. Repay the full amount on your payday, using the reference we give you.

Questions

Who can apply?

People with a salary paid into a bank account on a set payday.

Can I have more than one loan?

One loan at a time. A new loan is possible once the previous one is paid.

When do I repay?

In one payment on the payday we agree with you, after your salary is paid.

What happens to my documents?

We use them only to assess your application. If it is not approved, we delete your details and documents and let you know.
